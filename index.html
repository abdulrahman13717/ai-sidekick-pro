An AI assistant inside your browser — on any page

A browser extension with a side panel that understands the page you are reading: it summarizes, translates, answers, and writes with you. In Arabic and English.

Summarize & ask Any page in a few points, then ask about it
Instant translation Select any text and see it translated in place
Smart writing Polish your message or draft a reply in any text box
Read aloud & dictation Listen to a page, or speak and have it typed

How to get it

Subscription software (SaaS): install the extension from the Chrome Web Store, then your subscription issues a license key you enter once in Settings to unlock the paid features. Nothing is shipped and no human fulfilment is involved — activation is automatic via the key.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

First rule: nothing leaves your device before you explicitly agree. On first run the extension shows a screen naming everything that will leave and to whom, and it contacts no service until you press “I agree”.

Your chats, images and notes are stored on your device only (local browser storage). We do not upload them and we do not see them. The provider key you enter stays on your device and is sent only to its issuer.

What leaves the device, why, and to whom

Data To whom and why
Page text and what you select When you ask to explain or summarize it — to the AI model: Gemini or OpenRouter with your key, or our server if you have not added one
What you type in chat and smart writing To the same AI model
Images you attach To the same AI model
Text you translate To Google Translate
Your voice when you use the mic Your browser turns it into text through Google servers
A random number for your device that does not identify you To our server, to count your daily allowance
The IP address your request arrives from To our server, as a daily counter to prevent abuse — a count only, no content, deleted automatically after 24 hours

Our server does not store or log the text of your messages: it passes them to the provider and keeps only the two quota counters above, each expiring after 24 hours. The model provider may use what it receives to improve its services, so do not send what you do not want to share.

Limited Use

  • We use what we collect solely for the extension's stated purpose: answering, summarizing, translating, writing and reading aloud.
  • We do not sell or transfer your data to anyone except as the feature you requested requires (the provider you chose), or for legal compliance, or to protect against abuse.
  • No advertising and no ad targeting with your data, in any form.
  • No humans read your data. We do not review your chats or pages, and we do not store them on our server at all.

Permissions and why

  • Read the current page — to summarize, translate, and act on your selection.
  • Side panel and right-click menu — the extension's interface.
  • Local storage — your chats, settings and notes, on your device.
  • Text to speech — to read aloud what you ask for.

Your rights

Deleting everything about you is one action: removing the extension erases everything stored on your device. The quota counters on our server expire by themselves after 24 hours. For questions or complaints, the email is at the foot of this page.

The extension is not directed to anyone under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
